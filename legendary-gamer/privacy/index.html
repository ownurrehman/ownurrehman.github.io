Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers the Legendary Gamer website and Gaming HQ, the channel's own publishing tool. Gaming HQ is used only by the Legendary Gamer team to post our own gameplay videos to our own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What Gaming HQ uses

Gaming HQ uses YouTube API Services. When the channel owner connects the Legendary Gamer YouTube channel, Google asks for permission to:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to post our Shorts and videos to the channel.
  • Manage the channel's videos (youtube): to set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy and release times, add videos to our playlists, and read the channel's name and ID so posts only go to the right channel.

Gaming HQ does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers or other YouTube users, and it does not access any account other than the channel owner's.

What is stored and where

  • The Google access and refresh tokens, kept in a private file on our own server and never shared.
  • Our own video files, titles, descriptions and upload results (video IDs and their status).

Nothing is sold, shared with or shown to anyone outside the Legendary Gamer team, and nothing is used for advertising.

How long it is kept

Tokens are kept while the channel stays connected and are deleted when the channel is disconnected in Gaming HQ. Upload records are kept as the channel's own publishing history and can be deleted at any time.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, or by disconnecting YouTube in Gaming HQ.

Google and YouTube

By using a service built on YouTube API Services you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Other platforms

Gaming HQ also posts our videos to our own Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X accounts. It only sends our videos and their text and reads back the post links; it collects no data about other people on those platforms.

This website

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no analytics and no advertising cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub's own privacy statement applies to its servers.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message Legendary Gamer on YouTube or Facebook.
